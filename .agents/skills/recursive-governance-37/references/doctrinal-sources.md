# Canonical-source map and analogy boundaries

The engineering model is interpretive. Keep the Buddhist side anchored to early-discourse identifiers and do not present the software mapping as doctrine.

## Source priority

The normative Buddhist reference tier for this skill is early-discourse material (Nikāya / early Āgama where applicable). Machine-readable ontology and engineering mappings must retain provenance back to that tier. Later Buddhist / Mahāyāna vocabulary may be used only as an explicitly marked explanatory or UI dialect (upāya-style presentation adapter); it must not silently rewrite the early-discourse source layer.

## Primary source map

- **四念処** — MN 10 / DN 22: contemplation/observation of body, feeling, mind, and dhammas.
- **四正断 / 正精進** — SN 45.8 and parallels in the four-effort teachings: prevent unarisen unskillful states, abandon arisen unskillful states, develop unarisen skillful states, maintain/develop arisen skillful states.
- **四神足** — SN 51.20: bases of accomplishment/power associated with chanda, viriya, citta, and vīmaṃsā, with concentration and active effort.
- **五根** — SN 48.10: faith, energy, mindfulness, concentration/immersion, wisdom. The same discourse connects energy with the four efforts, mindfulness with the four establishments, concentration with the four jhānas, and wisdom with the four noble truths.
- **五根 / 五力 relation** — SN 48.43: faculties and powers are the same five qualities viewed under the two designations. In this skill, "capability" versus "robust capability" is an explicit engineering convention, not a canonical sequential doctrine.
- **七覚支** — SN 46.53: when the mind is sluggish, investigation, energy, and joy are suitable; when restless/agitated, tranquility, concentration, and equanimity are suitable; mindfulness remains useful. SN 46.51 supplies further conditions/nourishment for the factors.
- **八正道** — SN 45.8: definitions of right view, intention, speech, action, livelihood, effort, mindfulness, and concentration. MN 117 discusses their coordination and right concentration supported by the other factors.
- **四聖諦** — SN 56.11: suffering is to be understood; origin abandoned; cessation realized; path developed. The discourse also presents the three-turn/twelve-aspect structure used by this skill as an engineering state machine. The software labels Problem / Cause / Resolved State / Path remain interpretive.
- **念 / sati and recollection** — MN 53 and AN 5.14 describe mindfulness as remembering/recollecting what was said and done long ago. This supports the skill's engineering use of persistent episodic retention/recollection. It does not establish that the canonical term `sati` is identical to a modern computer-science model of episodic memory.
- **想 / saññā** — MN 43 explains saññā/perception through recognizing differentiating features (e.g. colors). This supports the recognition/labeling side of the engineering mapping. Graph construction, schemas, cue signatures, and episode indexing are explicit engineering extensions, not canonical definitions.
- **五蘊** — SN 22 discourses present form, feeling, perception, formations, and consciousness as the five aggregates; they are conditioned and are not to be taken as a permanent self. This skill therefore uses a five-aggregate snapshot as transient task/process state, not as an immutable agent persona or metaphysical identity.
- **四資具** — MN 2 and related texts discuss robes, almsfood, lodging, and medicine. Their mapping to identity/security envelope, information/compute input, runtime environment, and repair/recovery is analogy only; they are not the canonical definition of right livelihood.
- **四不壊浄** — discourses in SN 55 describe confidence in Buddha, Dhamma, Sangha, and ethical conduct. This skill deliberately uses those four as the protected fourfold basis inside 信根 and 信力: Buddha=Owner, Dhamma=Policy, Sangha=People/delegated authority, Sīla=Operating rules. This mapping is an engineering construction. It does not mean that SN 48.10 canonically defines saddhā-indriya as four software or organizational artifacts.

## Memory / imagination caution

The model distinguishes retained observation from reconstruction and imagination:

- `observed_episode` is historical evidence;
- `recalled_reconstruction` may fill only explicitly marked gaps;
- `counterfactual` and `prospective_simulation` are imagination used for testing.

The fact that recollection and imagination may share reconstruction machinery is an engineering hypothesis. Do not present imagined detail as remembered fact or as a canonical Buddhist claim.

## Five-aggregate orientation caution

The skill keeps a living user/task orientation and a Buddha/Dhamma canonical-source orientation. This is an engineering control choice. Do not claim the five aggregates canonically "point toward" a software user. The two orientations are kept explicit so optimization for the current user cannot erase doctrinal provenance, while doctrinal metaphor cannot create operational permission.

## Faith-model caution

The model makes two additional engineering conventions:

1. **信根** is treated as the capability to accurately reference what authorized humans explicitly established in the four trust anchors.
2. **信力** is treated as robust, principled derivation for cases not stated word-for-word, while remaining bounded by those anchors and never creating new authority or policy.

These are software-governance meanings built on the analogy. They should not be presented as canonical definitions of saddhā-indriya and saddhā-bala.

## Opposing-tendency caution

This skill pairs the five powers with the engineering failure tendencies **不信 / 懈怠 / 放逸 / 掉挙 / 無明**. The pairing defines robustness tests for AI autonomy; it is not a claim that these five terms are a canonical list identical to the five hindrances or that every early discourse defines the powers through exactly these opposites.

- 不信 resistance means principled derivation from protected human-authored anchors, not blind belief.
- 懈怠 resistance requires implementation through verified completion, not mere activity.
- 放逸 resistance requires sustained attention to observed fact and constraints.
- 掉挙 resistance requires objective preservation and sustained convergence under urgency or distraction.
- 無明 resistance requires distinguishing necessary and sufficient conditions and revising causal judgment under evidence.

## Translation caution

Japanese labels in the registry follow the user's requested traditional terminology. Pāli labels are included where stable and useful. Do not infer doctrinal equivalence from a software term merely because it shares a translated word such as "concentration", "effort", or "livelihood".
